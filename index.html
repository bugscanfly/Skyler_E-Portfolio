Carrier TransicoldApr 2026

Proposed redesign of a production-line continuity tester

The line's handheld continuity tester had contact pins that wobbled and sank during use. Operators had to stop and straighten them by hand, and good units sometimes failed. I proposed a structural redesign: a reinforced internal bracket, a plastic insulator to stop the pins sinking, spring-loaded pins on a ring lug connection, and an LED indicator on the handheld unit.

Energy Learning Journey exhibit: control hub and scale model

Control hub

I designed, wired and programmed an ESP32 control hub with a joystick, button, buzzer and zone LEDs, linking the physical model to a 3D model on screen. A second ESP32 in a touchscreen remote connects over ESP-NOW, so a presenter can run the exhibit from across the room. When the planned gesture sensor would not respond, I moved its function to the joystick.

Scale model, team lead

I led the team that designed and built a physical scale model of Singapore's power grid, covering generation, transmission and distribution, in one and a half weeks. We tried several approaches for the lighting and the moulded cooling towers before settling on the ones that worked, and delivered it on time for the Open House.
